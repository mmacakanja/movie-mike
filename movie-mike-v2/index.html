THE METHOD

Why five? Why seven?

A rating system should distinguish meaningful differences without pretending subjective judgments are laboratory measurements.

01

Why five categories?

Five categories capture the basic dimensions of what we look for in a film. Certain aspects of moviemaking are consolidated into categories to keep the ratings manageable.

02

Why a 0–7 scale?

Seven is the maximum number of meaningful differentiations we can make in a subjective judgment. Beyond that, we are splitting hairs and implying a precision that isn't really there.

03

What does a 7 mean?

A 7 is outstanding: best-in-class work in that category. On very rare occasions a film may earn an 8 for an accomplishment so extraordinary that it establishes a new standard for what movies can do.

THE MIKE SCALE

Compact on purpose.

The component ratings are integers. The aggregate can be a decimal because it is the average of five integer judgments.
